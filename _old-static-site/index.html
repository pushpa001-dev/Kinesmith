How it runs

Four steps. No surprises.

  1. 01

    Pilot

    Four videos, paid, fixed price. You see the real standard on your own footage before anything recurring is signed.

  2. 02

    System

    We build your motion kit — captions, lower thirds, transitions, sound. Every later asset inherits it.

  3. 03

    Cadence

    Fixed monthly volume, 24–48 hour turnaround, two revision rounds per stage. Same two people every month.

  4. 04

    Read

    We go through retention curves and hook drop-off with you, then cut the next batch against what actually held.
